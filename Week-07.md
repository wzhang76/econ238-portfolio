# Week 7: The Orchard Stream Buffer Initiative

## Proposition Title

The Orchard Stream Buffer Initiative

## The Ballot Question

Shall Wayne County support a three-year pilot inviting up to five apple orchards to replace 5,000 feet of streamside apple trees with 35-foot-wide native-plant buffers? Installation would be capped at $50,000. The county would coordinate a competitive grant application covering 75% of eligible costs; participating growers would pay 25% and maintain the buffers. A YES vote supports this pilot only if funding and willing farms are secured.
